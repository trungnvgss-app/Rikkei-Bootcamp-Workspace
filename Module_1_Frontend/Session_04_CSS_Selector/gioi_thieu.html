Giới thiệu dự án

Đây là trang giới thiệu của website 3D với giao diện đồng bộ, hiện đại và khoa học. Nội dung trong ảnh đã được dẫn vào menu để người dùng nhận ra phong cách thiết kế ngay từ đầu.

A website (also written as a web site) is one or more web pages and related content that is identified by a common domain name and published on at least one web server. Websites are typically dedicated to a particular topic or purpose, such as news, entertainment, or social media. Hyperlinking between web pages guides the navigation of the site, which often starts with a home page. The most-visited sites are Google, YouTube, and Facebook.

All publicly-accessible websites collectively constitute the World Wide Web. There are also private networks, such as a company’s internal website for its employees. Users can access websites on a range of devices, including desktops, laptops, tablets, and smartphones. The app used on these devices is called a web browser.
